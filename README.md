# Neon Tide: Palmira Bay

An original open-world crime game built for make.fun's [GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01). Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car: steal and drive anything with wheels, run **four story jobs** for the Solace family — the last one a timed pickup-and-deliver clear across the map — and buy back your name.

**The city** is a pastel avenue grid with striped crosswalks at every junction, billboards over the avenues, palms and lamps, a green park block (Palmira Green) with shade trees and benches, a beach where pedestrians stroll the sand, and a pier reaching into the bay.

**The law** is a five-star wanted system: patrol cars hunt and ram, and at four stars a **search helicopter** circles overhead — while its spotlight is on you, your wanted level cannot cool down. Cars flash their **brake lights** when they slow, traffic queues and turns, and citizens flee gunfire and remember what they saw.

**On foot**, sprinting drains a stamina meter (shown on the HUD) — empty it and you are winded until it recovers. The HUD carries a live minimap, an objective card, a circular speed badge while driving, and a red damage vignette when Vex takes a hit. A synthesized bassline (WebAudio, no audio assets) starts on your first input — **N** toggles it, **Esc** pauses. Engine, siren, gunfire and crash sounds are synthesized too. Progress saves between visits; everything is static files under `game/`, with three.js vendored and the rest hand-written for this entry.
