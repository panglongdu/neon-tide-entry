# Neon Tide: Palmira Bay

An original open-world crime game built for make.fun's [GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01). Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car: steal and drive anything with wheels, run three story jobs for the Solace family, outrun a five-star wanted system with patrol cars that hunt and ram, and buy back your name. Traffic, pedestrians, a live minimap, synthesized engine and siren sounds, and progress that saves between visits — all in static files under `game/`, with three.js vendored and everything else hand-written for this entry.
